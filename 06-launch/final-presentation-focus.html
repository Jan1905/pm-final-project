Slide 10 · Story

The key learning: clarity before self-service

Where I started

I assumed customers needed more self-service features. The interviews and data showed that the bigger problem was understanding the payment commitment.

Aha moment

Customers were not asking for more actions. They were asking for more clarity.

Key takeaways
  • Confusion was the root problem.
  • Interviews and data told the same story.
  • Clear information beat more controls.
What I would do next
  • Run the pilot.
  • Review the remaining support contacts.
  • Improve explanations for agreement changes.
  • Add self-service only after validation.

# GTM Launch Plan, Riverty business-unit case

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Installment Agreement Summary Experience |
| Goal | Conversion |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Conversion, The goal is to reduce post-creation manual contacts by helping Responsible Payers better understand their installment agreement. Research and operational data showed that customers frequently require additional clarification after agreement creation because key information such as payment responsibility, financed amount, remaining balance, and remaining obligation is fragmented or difficult to understand. The Installment Agreement Summary directly addresses this pain point by providing a single, transparent view of the customer's financial commitment, making conversion the most appropriate launch goal.
- **Target audience:** Primary audience:
Responsible Payers (parents, patients, or self-paying customers) with an active installment agreement.

Secondary audience:
Customer Service, Account Management, and Operations teams who support customers with installment agreement questions and are expected to benefit from reduced contact volume.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, The Installment Agreement Summary is an enhancement to an existing installment agreement experience rather than a new product launch. The primary objective is to reduce post-creation manual contacts by improving transparency and customer understanding. The target audience is limited to Responsible Payers using installment agreements, making a focused communication approach more appropriate than a broad multi-channel launch. The greatest risk of silence is low adoption and missed business impact rather than lack of market awareness.

## Channels
1. **Owned: Installment agreement portal / customer self-service area. The new Installment Agreement Summary is presented directly where Responsible Payers already manage and review their installment agreements.**
2. **Owned: Transactional email communication. Include a direct link to the Installment Agreement Summary in agreement confirmation, welcome, and account-related emails to drive awareness and usage.**
3. **Owned: Customer Service and support interactions. Agents proactively guide customers to the Installment Agreement Summary during agreement-related inquiries and use it as the primary reference point for payment obligation questions.**

## Enablement & Assets
- Installment Agreement Summary UI
- Customer-facing FAQ explaining the summary
- One-page Customer Service guide
- Internal support training materials
- Standard response templates referencing the summary
- Updated help-center content
- Product overview for Operations and Customer Service teams
- KPI dashboard to track post-creation manual contact rate and direct debit return rate

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** Product Manager owns the launch plan, success metrics, and post-launch evaluation.

Designer owns the Installment Agreement Summary experience, UI assets, and customer-facing guidance content.

Engineering owns implementation, quality assurance, rollout, and experiment setup.

Customer Service Lead owns support enablement, FAQ creation, agent briefing, and feedback collection.

Operations owns KPI monitoring and reporting of post-creation manual contact rate and direct debit return rate.

No additional marketing budget is required. Existing owned channels (customer portal, transactional emails, and support interactions) will be used.
- **Timeline:** Phase 1 – Pilot Preparation (Weeks 1–2)
- Finalize design and prototype
- Complete implementation and QA
- Create FAQ and Customer Service materials
- Configure experiment tracking and reporting

Phase 2 – Pilot Launch (Weeks 3–6)
- Launch A/B test to Responsible Payers
- 50/50 traffic split
- Monitor experiment health, contact rate, and guardrail metric
- Collect Customer Service feedback

Phase 3 – Evaluation & Rollout Decision (Week 7)
- Analyze experiment results
- Validate statistical significance
- Compare manual contact rate and direct debit return rate against targets
- Make go/no-go decision for broader rollout

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** 1. Post-creation manual contact rate decreases from 14.6% to 13.1% or lower.

2. Installment Agreement Summary view rate reaches at least 70% of eligible Responsible Payers.

3. Direct debit return rate remains at or below the current baseline of 13.0%.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Customers view the Installment Agreement Summary, but the post-creation manual contact rate does not improve. This would indicate that the feature increases visibility without actually improving understanding of the payment obligation.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Iterate.  If contact rates improve but the target reduction is not fully achieved, use customer feedback and behavioral data to identify remaining confusion points and improve the Installment Agreement Summary experience before broader rollout.

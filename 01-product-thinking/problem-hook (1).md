# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** My own initiative

## Strategic crisis
As regulatory requirements, product variants, and market demands continue to grow, BFS Health Finance risks increasing complexity, slower time-to-market, and rising delivery costs because core capabilities are repeatedly implemented across multiple vertical solutions.

## Moment of misery
Businesses must manage multiple financing offerings across products, markets, and regulatory frameworks, creating operational complexity and manual effort. Meanwhile, end customers experience inconsistent financing journeys, making it harder to understand, access, and trust available payment options.

## Problem hook
Every new product, regulatory change, or market opportunity increases complexity because core capabilities are duplicated across multiple vertical solutions, making innovation slower and more expensive.

## Value proposition
For Orthodontic practices, driving schools, and other healthcare and education service providers that need flexible, compliant financing solutions for their customers., we will Build a shared financing platform with reusable core capabilities that enables tailored solutions for multiple verticals, reducing complexity while accelerating the delivery of new products and regulatory changes. because Regulatory change and market disruption are creating a unique window of opportunity. Customers are actively re-evaluating financing providers, and organizations that can scale solutions quickly across verticals will be best positioned to win and retain market share..

## Cold-read self-review
The strongest part is the urgency: CCD2 is forcing practices to make financing decisions now. The weakest part is that the customer pain is less concrete than the business opportunity. I would strengthen the hook by focusing more on the challenges practices face in maintaining patient financing. The risk is high-stakes, and the problem is systemic because it affects an entire market segment rather than a niche use case.
